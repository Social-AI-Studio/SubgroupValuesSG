# Data licence

The code is released under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`). The model outputs (`results/raw_inference/`), the anonymised human-evaluation labels (`data/human_eval/`) and the generated tables (`outputs/tables/`) are released under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode), subject to:

- **World Values Survey.** The dataset is recreated from the WVS Wave 7 Singapore file, which you obtain from [worldvaluessurvey.org](https://www.worldvaluessurvey.org/); it and the recreated files are subject to the WVS conditions of use. Please cite the WVS.
- **WorldValuesBench.** `data/codebook.json` and `data/question_metadata.json` are adapted from [WorldValuesBench](https://github.com/Demon702/WorldValuesBench) (Zhao et al., 2024).
- **Model outputs.** The terms of the respective model providers also apply.
- **Human evaluation.** Annotator identities have been removed (A1–A3).
